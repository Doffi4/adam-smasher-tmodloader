0.1.4 — hard boss combat rework.
Each burst re-aims with a fixed final warning; Sandevistan dashes pass through the marked target, with safe recovery between attacks.
Three/five marked missile strike points; rockets deliver bounded impact blasts instead of surprise contact hits. Phase two chains missile strikes into a separately warned gun burst.
Boss base life 18 million, contact 600, bullets 350 and blasts 450 before difficulty, player defense and other mod hooks. Preliminary balance.
New outlined hostile missile/bolt sprites, trails and impact effects; boss warning lines sample only a 1x1 pixel of MagicPixel.
Weapons from 0.1.3, reusable summon, boss reward and independent 50/50 drop preserved. Server and all clients must use 0.1.4.

0.1.3 — fix oversized magic VFX and reduce mana consumption.
Sample only a 1x1 pixel of Terraria's 1x1000 MagicPixel strip: trails, arcs and flashes no longer stretch into screen-spanning bars.
Halve base mana per salvo/charge payment from 20 to 10. Equipment discounts and regeneration remain supported. Base right-click total is 40 mana for a one-second charge, 70 for two seconds.
Melee ramp, damage, attack cadence, boss and independent 50/50 reward are preserved.

0.1.2 — held melee ramp and animated magic effects.
Hold left click: mantis slash damage rises by up to 50% over two seconds; reach grows from 175 to 315 pixels. Release, right click, item switch or interrupted input resets the ramp.
Magic missiles gain trails and flashes; the plasma field grows, rotates, pulses and fades. Gameplay mana costs remain unchanged: 20 base mana per salvo, reduced by equipment.
Boss, summon recipe and independent 50/50 weapon reward are unchanged.
All clients and the server must use the same version. Preliminary balance; GPU appearance, live multiplayer transport and equipped DPS comparisons remain unverified.
